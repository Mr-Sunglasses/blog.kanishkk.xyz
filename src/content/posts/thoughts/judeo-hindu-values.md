Unlike many parts of the world, Jews were never systematically persecuted in India, and they were able to practice Judaism openly while becoming an important part of Indian society. Even today, many Israelis travel to India and are welcomed warmly by local communities. India is one of the most safest places for Israeli tourists to travel.

The bond between many Hindus and Jews comes from a deep understanding of history, resilience, and survival through centuries of challenges.
